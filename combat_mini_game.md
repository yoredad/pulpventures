combat mini game
tap battle
villain has 10 hp, player has 10hp
randomly a fist appears every 0.8 seconds for 0.2 second duration
player must tap fist while displayed, if the fist is hit then a target appears in a random location immediately for 0.2 second duration.
if the player misses the fist they lose 1 hp of damage
if the player taps a target the villain loses 1 hp of damage
play continues until either the villain hp = 0 or the player hp = 0
before the game starts perform a strength roll (random number between 1 and 20)
if the number is <= to the player strength stat add 0.2 seconds to the fist and target durations
a roll of 1 is an auto fail and a roll of 20 is auto success
